## 5. Scorecard: Is Your Website Losing You Work?

- 12 questions about the Google profile, website and follow-up, about 3 minutes.
- Q0 (not scored) asks the business type: sign and print, HVAC, roofing, other trade, other service. It's used for the `industry-{value}` tag.
- Each question scores 2 / 1 / 0 points; 24 max.

| # | Question | Fix (if 0 or 1) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do you show up in the map results for your main service and area? | Tune your profile's categories, services and service area. |
| 2 | Is your profile's main category the exact service that earns you the most? | Set the main category to your top-earning service. |
| 3 | When did you get your last Google review? | Ask after every job, with a link or QR code. |
| 4 | Do you ask every customer for a review? | Put the request in the tool you already use. |
| 5 | Do you reply to your Google reviews? | Reply to every review; templates make it fast. |
| 6 | Does your profile have recent photos of your actual work? | Add real job photos monthly. |
| 7 | Can someone call or request a quote from your homepage in one tap on a phone? | Add a one-tap call button and a short quote form. |
| 8 | Does your website have its own page for each main service? | Give your top service its own page. |
| 9 | Does the first line of your homepage say what you do and where? | Say what you do and where in the first line. |
| 10 | After a job, do past customers hear from you again? | A simple check-in brings repeat work and referrals. |
| 11 | Do you know how many calls or quote requests you got last month? | Check and save your profile numbers monthly. |
| 12 | Do you ask new customers how they found you? | Add "How did you hear about us?" to your form. |

| Points | Band | Headline |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Losing work | You're probably losing calls to the shop down the street. |
| 9–16 | Leaking calls | You're doing some things right, but calls are slipping through. |
| 17–24 | Built to win work | You're set up to win work. |

- The result shows up to 3 fixes: answers below 2, lowest first, ties by question order. A perfect score shows "No leaks found."
- The result button leads to the free Website Review.
- The email goes from johnpaul@ via Gmail and is saved to the Google Sheet (`Leads` tab).

## 7. Measurement rules (Google profile data)

- **Google's limits:** the Performance view shows about 6 months; downloads cover 90 days per report; the API returns up to 18 months.
- **Day 0, before any change:**
  - Screenshot the performance view
  - Export the last 6 months in 90-day chunks
  - Record the review count and rating
  - Screenshot map results for 3 money searches from 3 spots
- **Days 30, 60 and 90:** repeat the captures and compare same-length periods.
- **Growth Advisor:** export monthly so history grows past 6 months.
- **Claims:** call clicks, reviews and map position are signs of progress. Claim jobs only from "How did you hear about us?" answers.
