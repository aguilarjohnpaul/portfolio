## 2. Audience

**Primary buyer:** the owner of a B2B or local service business with 5 to 20 people. Most new work comes from referrals, and referrals have slowed.
- Industries: agencies, trades, accounting and bookkeeping, IT services, consultancies, clinics, design studios.
- Their words: "referrals slowed down," "word of mouth," "leads go quiet," "busy one month, empty the next," "no time to follow up."

**The three pains (homepage):**
1. **Referrals slowed down.** Word of mouth used to keep you busy. Lately it's quieter, and you're not sure why.
2. **Leads go quiet.** People reach out, you send a quote, and then nothing. No one has time to chase.
3. **Busy, then empty.** One month you're turning work away. The next, you're wondering where it went.

**Secondary audiences**
- Startup founders. No dedicated page in Phase 1. They can take the scorecard and apply as founding clients.
- Design studios of 2–10 people. They get the White-label Strategy Sprint on `/studios`, which is linked from the footer only.

## 5. Scorecard (lead magnet) details

**Flow:** intro → 12 questions, one per screen → the score, band and biggest leak, shown free → an email form for the full breakdown (all three stage scores plus the top 3 fixes).

**Answer scoring:** Always = 3 · Usually = 2 · Sometimes = 1 · Rarely or never = 0. Q5 has its own options: Within the hour = 3 · Same day = 2 · Within 1–2 days = 1 · Longer = 0.

| # | Stage | Question | Fix (given when the answer scores 1 or less) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get referred | When a project wraps up well, do you ask the client for a referral? | Ask at the moment of praise. Add one line to your wrap-up call: "Who else do you know who deals with this?" |
| 2 | Get referred | Do your clients know exactly what kind of client you'd like referred? | Write a one-sentence "ideal referral" description and put it in your email signature and wrap-up message. |
| 3 | Get referred | Do other businesses (partners, vendors, peers) send you work regularly? | List five businesses that serve your clients before or after you. Offer each a simple two-way referral arrangement. |
| 4 | Get referred | Do you thank or reward people who refer you? | Send a handwritten note or a small thank-you within 48 hours of every referral, whether or not it turns into work. |
| 5 | Get hired | How fast do you usually reply to a new inquiry? | Set an auto-reply that confirms receipt and books a call slot. Aim to reply personally the same business day. |
| 6 | Get hired | Do you follow up on quotes or proposals that haven't been answered? | Follow up on day 2, day 7 and day 14 with a short, useful note, not a "just checking in." |
| 7 | Get hired | Can a new prospect tell what you offer and roughly what it costs from your website? | Add a plain "What we do and starting prices" section. Clear ranges filter out poor fits and speed up yeses. |
| 8 | Get hired | Do you track where each new lead came from? | Add "How did you hear about us?" to your inquiry form and log the answer for every lead. |
| 9 | Get hired again | Do you check in with past clients after the work ends? | Put a 30-, 90- and 180-day check-in on your calendar for every finished project. |
| 10 | Get hired again | When a project ends, do you offer a clear next step or service? | Write one natural follow-on offer for your most common project and mention it at wrap-up. |
| 11 | Get hired again | Do you ask happy clients for reviews or testimonials? | Send a review link with two prompt questions within a week of a strong finish. |
| 12 | Get hired again | Do you know which clients have referred you before? | Keep a simple list of referrers and what they sent. Your best referrers deserve the most attention. |

**Math**
- Stage score = the stage's 4 answers ÷ 12 × 100, rounded.
- Total = all 12 answers ÷ 36 × 100, rounded.
- Weakest stage = the lowest stage score. Ties go to the earlier stage.
- Top 3 fixes = questions scoring 1 or less, sorted by score and then question order. If there are fewer than 3, fill with score-2 questions from the weakest stage.
- On-screen fix = the lowest-scoring question in the weakest stage.

| Score | Band | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0–39 | Running on luck | Work comes in when it comes in. A few basics would change that fast. |
| 40–69 | Working, but leaking | Referrals happen, but you're losing good work at one or two stages. |
| 70–100 | Built to be referred | Your system works. The gains now come from new lead sources and consistency. |

**What gets saved in Kit**
- Custom fields: `score_total`, `score_referred`, `score_hired`, `score_again`, `band`, `weakest_stage`, `top_fixes` (joined by `||`), `team_size`, `business_name`, `hero_variant`.
- Tags: `scorecard-complete`, `band-<slug>`, `weakest-<slug>`, `hero-a|b`, `team-5-20`.
- The results email ends with an invitation to the founding program.

- 70+ → Lead Growth Roadmap.

## 7. Voice rules for 21 Jewel copy

- Use plain words an owner would use: referrals, word of mouth, leads, follow-up, steady work, slow months.
- Avoid: RevOps, GTM, funnel optimization, synergy, leverage, "unlock," "supercharge," "seamless."
- Short sentences, active voice, second person ("you"). JP uses "I" on the founding, about and studios pages.
- No invented stats, client logos or testimonials. Missing facts stay as `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
- No emoji. Go light on em dashes.
- Palette "Evergreen": ink `#1E3B2F`, ground `#F3EFE6`, surface `#FBF9F6`, accent `#B5643C` (fills only), accent text `#9F5835`. Headings in Fraunces, body in Inter.
